Backup and restore

Daily midnight backup email

A web page cannot send email by itself while the phone or computer is off. This option uses a free Google Apps Script in your Google account. The app sends it your latest data a few seconds after every change, and the script emails the backup file every night between 12 and 1 AM.

One-time setup steps and script
  1. Open script.google.com, click New project, and in Project settings set your time zone (India Standard Time).
  2. Paste the script below, change CHANGE_ME to a secret of your own, and save.
  3. Choose setup in the function list and click Run once. Approve the permissions. This creates the midnight trigger.
  4. Click Deploy, New deployment, type Web app, Execute as Me, Who has access Anyone. Copy the web app URL.
  5. Paste the URL and the same secret above, click Save, then press Email backup now to test.
